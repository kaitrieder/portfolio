About

I am a Post-Baccalaureate Fellow in the Blackmon Lab at Texas A&M University, studying the evolution of sex chromosomes through comparative genomics and phylogenetic methods.

Evolutionary Geneticist & Genomics Researcher

Comparative genomics of pseudoautosomal region (PAR) size and chromosomal evolution, using phylogenetic comparative methods to study sex chromosome evolution across the tree of life.

I earned my B.S. in Cellular and Molecular Biology from Trinity University, where I was a Ronald E. McNair Scholar. My background spans neuroscience and behavioral research as well as evolutionary and comparative genomics, and I enjoy working with incomplete data to bridge gaps in our understanding of chromosomal evolution.
